## Full description (max 4000)

```
Money Made turns the transaction SMS your bank already sends into a clear picture of your money — automatically, and completely privately.

HOW IT WORKS
When your bank or UPI app sends an SMS like "Rs.450 debited … to VPA swiggy@icici", Money Made records it as "Swiggy · Food · ₹450". No typing, no linking bank accounts, no sign-up.

• Reads transaction alerts from most Indian banks and UPI apps
• Ignores OTPs, offers, reminders and personal messages
• Sorts spending into categories like Food, Travel, Shopping, Bills and Subscriptions
• Learns from you: fix a category once and choose "Always use this" for that merchant
• Never counts the same payment twice, and recognises transfers between your own accounts

SEE WHERE YOUR MONEY GOES
• Monthly overview of money received and spent
• Daily and weekly spending trends, and where it went by category
• Estimated spending for the month, based on your pace so far
• Analytics for the week, month, 3 months, 6 months and the year
• Your highest spending days, each one tap away from the details

STAY IN CONTROL
• Filter by date, category, payment method and bank account
• Edit merchants, add notes, exclude items from totals
• "Needs review" collects anything the app couldn't categorise
• Export your transactions to CSV whenever you like

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything is processed and stored only on your phone
• No account, no servers, no ads, no analytics, no trackers
• The app doesn't even request internet access
• Only the last digits of account numbers are kept
• Delete your data anytime — or erase everything in one tap

Money Made asks for SMS permission only to find transaction alerts, and explains exactly why before asking. You can use it without SMS access, pause detection, or turn off individual bank senders at any time.
```
